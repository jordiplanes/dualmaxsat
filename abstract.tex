\begin{abstract}
This memo combines five notes on Implicit Hitting Set (IHS) methods for
MaxSAT and Weighted CSP. Part~1 frames the algorithmic landscape. It models
core-guided MaxSAT as a Logic-Based Benders Decomposition, and IHS as
Dantzig--Wolfe column generation on the dual core-packing LP, separating
structural features that transfer from those that do not.

Part~2 sharpens this claim with a worked example showing exact equivalence at the LP level,
and a three-clause counterexample showing that the integer master used by
practical solvers is strictly stronger than Dantzig--Wolfe. Part~3 provides
a broader taxonomy of IHS--OR relationships, mapping the algorithm to
Lagrangian relaxation, dual ascent, and cutting-plane methods. Part~4 reads the
32-configuration empirical study of \citet{petrova2025empirical} on IHS for
weighted CSPs through the same OR lens, confirming the qualitative
predictions of Parts~1--3 while reordering the relative importance of the
design axes. Part~5 places multivalued Regular MaxSAT between Boolean MaxSAT
and Weighted CSP, showing that the hitting-set master gains only a
gap-neutral block of order constraints per variable, so the Dantzig--Wolfe
duality survives multivaluedness intact.
\end{abstract}
